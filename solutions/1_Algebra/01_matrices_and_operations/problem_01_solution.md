### Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

**Problem**

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

1. State the sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$.
2. Read off the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$.
3. Write the second row of $A$ and the first column of $B$ as vectors.

**Solution**

**Part 1: Sizes of Matrices**

- The matrix $A$ has $2$ rows and $3$ columns, so its size is $2 \times 3$.
- The matrix $B$ has $3$ rows and $2$ columns, so its size is $3 \times 2$.

**Part 2: Specific Entries**

- $a_{12}$ is the entry in the first row and second column of $A$:

$$
a_{12} = -1
$$

- $a_{23}$ is the entry in the second row and third column of $A$:

$$
a_{23} = 5
$$

- $b_{21}$ is the entry in the second row and first column of $B$:

$$
b_{21} = -2
$$

- $b_{32}$ is the entry in the third row and second column of $B$:

$$
b_{32} = 1
$$

**Part 3: Row and Column Vectors**

- The second row of $A$ expressed as a vector:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

- The first column of $B$ expressed as a vector:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4
\end{pmatrix}
$$
