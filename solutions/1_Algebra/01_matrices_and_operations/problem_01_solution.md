### Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

Given

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

and

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

---

#### 1. Sizes of Matrices $A$ and $B$

The size of a matrix with $m$ rows and $n$ columns is denoted as $m \times n$.

Matrix $A$ has $2$ rows and $3$ columns. Thus, the size of $A$ is $2 \times 3$.

Matrix $B$ has $3$ rows and $2$ columns. Thus, the size of $B$ is $3 \times 2$.

---

#### 2. Matrix Entries

* $a_{12} = -1$
* $a_{23} = 5$
* $b_{21} = -2$
* $b_{32} = 1$

---

#### 3. Row and Column Vectors

The second row of matrix $A$ as a row vector is:

$$
r_2(A) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}
$$

The first column of matrix $B$ as a column vector is:

$$
c_1(B)=\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
